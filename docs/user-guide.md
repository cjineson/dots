# User guide

## Dashboard
- Setlists can sit at the top level or inside a one-level **group**. New and copied setlists appear at the top.
- When all setlists are collapsed, a ☰ handle appears on each setlist; long-press and drag it to reorder within its group (or the top level). The top-level + offers "Add new setlist" or "Add new setlist from existing" (a copy). Songs and breaks inside a setlist are reordered by long-pressing and dragging their ☰ handles.
- When a setlist has a location, a small map-pin beside its location/date line opens the venue in Maps.
- Expand a setlist to see its songs and breaks. Per-setlist buttons: play (opens the whole setlist), edit details, add (+: new song, copy song from another setlist, or break), delete (−).
- Song rows: tap the title to open Performance; edit, copy to another setlist, and remove (−).
- A **backup banner** appears when you have setlists and have not exported for 30 days (or ever). **Remind me later** hides it for 3 days, **Dismiss** for 30.
- The bottom bar has the **Settings** button.

## Removing songs
Songs only exist inside setlists, and editing a song updates it everywhere it appears.
- If the song is in other setlists, removing it just takes it out of this one.
- If it is in no other setlist you choose **Keep ungrouped** (moves it to an "Ungrouped songs" setlist outside any group) or **Remove permanently**.

## Setlist details
- Name, group (or create one inline), date and time (native pickers), and a **location**.
- Location can be typed, filled from **Use current location**, or looked up with **Find on map**. A map position is stored with the setlist, shown as saved coordinates, and can be cleared. **Open in Maps** launches the platform map app.
- **Notes / checklist**: free text. Lines starting `[ ]` or `[x]` become tickable items in Flow.

## Song editor
Title, coloured tags (with quick picks of tags already in your library), cue sections, styled cue blocks, editable tables, and a **Preview performance view** that returns to the unsaved editor. New songs start at the default text size from Settings.

Leaving any editor (song, setlist, group) with unsaved changes prompts **Keep editing / Discard / Save**.

## Flow view
A vertically scrolling view of the whole setlist, with notes/checklist at the top.
Header buttons (panels are mutually exclusive):
- **Auto-scroll** – play/pause plus a speed slider; dragging the page stops it.
- **Tag** – global tag size slider.
- **Zoom** – "set entire setlist" text size; each song header has its own zoom button.

The GigBox logo is the "home" button and fills the header height.

## Performance view
One cue box per song; long lines scale down to fit unless you set a manual size. Swipe left/right for next/previous (sensitivity configurable). The header shows the song title, tags and optionally "3 of 12". Tag size and zoom buttons work as in Flow, and the screen can stay awake.
