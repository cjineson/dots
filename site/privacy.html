Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Dots is designed to keep your data on your device. This is a draft: review it against the final app, and get legal advice if you need it.

What we collect

Nothing. Dots has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not send your songs, setlists or settings to us or to any third party.

Where your data is stored

Your songs, setlists and settings are stored in a database on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Backups are files you export yourself and keep wherever you choose.

Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Use current location" on a setlist, Dots asks for permission and uses your position once to suggest a venue name. The result is saved with the setlist on your device only. Looking up a typed address or a position uses Apple's location services, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Maps links

Tapping a setlist location opens it in your maps app. What your maps app does with that is covered by its own privacy policy.

Children

Dots does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
