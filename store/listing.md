## Description
GigBox is a stage-ready setlist and cue viewer for working musicians.

Build your setlists, drop in your lyrics, chords and notes, then tap a title to go live. Big, clear text, swipe between songs, and a screen that stays awake.

PERFORMANCE VIEW
- Large, readable cues that scale to fit the screen
- Swipe between songs, or turn swiping off
- Auto-scroll with a slow "crawl" control for long songs
- Screen stays awake while you play

FLOW VIEW
- Scroll a whole setlist end to end, with tickable checklist notes for load-in and kit

SETLISTS THAT MATCH REAL GIGS
- Venue, date and time on every setlist, with a tap to open the venue in Maps
- Group setlists into folders
- Reorder songs by dragging, add breaks, reuse a song across setlists
- Colour-coded cues and tags

YOURS, AND OFFLINE
- Everything is stored on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking
- Export a JSON backup and import it on another device
- Import notes from Apple Notes

MAKE IT YOURS
- Palettes: Stage, Pretty, Aqua, Chill, Beige and Goth, each with dark and light variants
- Adjustable text sizes, high-contrast mode and more

## Icon
1024 x 1024 PNG, no transparency, no rounded corners (iOS rounds it). Suggested concept: a stage-lit box or a setlist page with a spotlight, using the existing GigBox logo colours.

## App Review notes
GigBox needs no login. It works entirely offline and stores data on the device. To test, tap "Create your first setlist", add a song, then tap the setlist title's play button to open Performance view. Location is optional and is only used to fill in a venue name when the user taps "Use current location".
